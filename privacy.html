Privacy Policy for Mandi Typist

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

Mandi Typist is an educational typing speed practice tool designed for wholesale vegetable markets, grocery stores, and billing clerks. We believe your data belongs exclusively to you.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Mandi Typist does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information or keystroke data.

  • Local Storage Only: All your typing speeds, practice statistics, personal best scores, and custom vegetable names are stored locally on your own device using standard web browser local storage (localStorage).
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the application.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app does not include third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, or telemetry SDKs.

3. Offline Capabilities

Mandi Typist functions as a Progressive Web App (PWA). All application assets, fonts, sounds, and vegetable databases are cached on your device to enable 100% offline usage without an active internet connection.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Mandi Typist does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.
